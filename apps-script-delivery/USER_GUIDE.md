# ដឹកជញ្ជូន · Delivery app: user guide

Open **https://isteel-app.pages.dev** → tile **ដឹកជញ្ជូន**. The app is in Khmer; the 🇰🇭/🇬🇧 button switches to English.
Bottom bar: ទំព័រដើម (Home), ផ្ទុកថ្មី (Refresh), សង្ខេប (Summary), កត់ត្រា (Record), ជើងដឹក (Trips), DN, ផ្សេងៗ (More).

## ខ្មែរ

**ជើងដឹក និង DN.** រាល់ពេល ចូលថ្លឹង បង្កើតលេខជើងដឹកថ្មី (ឧ. 26-10-12-008)។ ឡានមួយអាចមានច្រើនជើងក្នុងមួយថ្ងៃ។
ជើងមួយអាចដឹក DN ច្រើន ហើយ DN មួយអាចបែងចែកដឹកច្រើនជើង។ ជំហាននីមួយៗកត់ជាជួរថ្មី មិនសរសេរជាន់លើទេ។

1. **សន្តិសុខ – ចូលថ្លឹង:** កត់ត្រា → ១.ចូលថ្លឹង → ជ្រើស ឬវាយផ្លាកលេខ (ឡានជួល/អតិថិជន វាយបាន) → តៃកុង → រូបថត (មិនចាំបាច់) → កត់ត្រា។ អេក្រង់បង្ហាញលេខជើងដឹក។
2. **ភស្តុភារ – ២.ដល់កន្លែងចែកបុង:** ជ្រើសជើងដឹក → បន្ថែម DN ពី Delivery Plan, វាយលេខ, ឬចុច 📷 ស្កេនបាកូដលើបុង។ DN មិនមានក្នុងផែនការ ត្រូវជ្រើសអតិថិជន។ បន្ទាប់មក ៣.ចេញពីកន្លែងចែកបុង។
3. **ឃ្លាំង – ៤, ៥, ៦:** ៦.ឡើងទំនិញរួចរាល់ ត្រូវជ្រើសឃ្លាំង ទំងន់ Websling និងរូបថតទំនិញ។
4. **សន្តិសុខ – ៧.ចេញពីកន្លែងថ្លឹង។**
5. **តៃកុង – ៨, ៩:** នៅទំព័រជើងដឹក ចុច ▦ QR ឬ ចែករំលែកតំណ ទៅតៃកុង។ តៃកុងបើកតំណ (មិនចាំបាច់ចូលគណនី) → ជ្រើស DN → ៨.មកដល់ ឬ ៩.បានប្រគល់។
   ប្រគល់មួយផ្នែក: ធីក "ប្រគល់តែមួយផ្នែក" ហើយវាយទំងន់។ DN ដែលនៅសល់ ដឹកនៅជើងបន្ទាប់ (ឡានចូលថ្លឹងម្តងទៀត → ជើងថ្មី)។
6. **x.ការដឹកជញ្ជូនបរាជ័យ / x.លុបចោល:** ត្រូវសរសេរមូលហេតុ។

ប្រសិនបើកម្មវិធីបង្ហាញ ⚠️ "សូមពិនិត្យ" អានហើយជ្រើស **បាទ/ចាស កត់ត្រា** ឬ **ទេ ត្រលប់**។
គ្មានអ៊ីនធឺណិត: ជំហានរក្សាទុកក្នុងទូរស័ព្ទ (⏳ នៅខាងលើ) ហើយផ្ញើដោយខ្លួនឯងពេលមានសេវា។
**Supervisor/Admin:** លុបការកត់ត្រាពីទំព័រជើងដឹក (🗑 លុប + មូលហេតុ → Deleted Records និង Audit Log; មិនអាចកែម៉ោងទេ), ពិនិត្យគុណភាពទិន្នន័យ, Admin កំណត់តួនាទីអ្នកប្រើ។

## English

**Trips and DNs.** Every Gate In makes a new Trip ID (e.g. 26-10-12-008); one truck can make several trips a day.
A trip can carry many DNs and one DN can be split over several trips. Each step is a new row; nothing is overwritten.

1. **Security – Gate In:** Record → ១.ចូលថ្លឹង → choose or type the plate (rented/customer trucks can be typed) → driver → photo (optional) → Record. The Trip ID is shown.
2. **Logistics – Arrival-Pickup DN:** choose the trip → add DNs from the Delivery Plan, by typing, or 📷 scan the Delivery Note barcode. A DN not in the plan needs the customer. Then Departure-Pickup.
3. **Warehouse – steps 4, 5, 6:** End Loading asks for the warehouse, weight, websling and a goods photo.
4. **Security – Gate Out.**
5. **Driver – Arrived / Delivered:** on the trip screen tap ▦ QR or Share link. The driver opens it (no sign-in), ticks the DN and records Arrived or Delivered.
   Part delivery: tick "Only part delivered" and type the kg. The rest goes on the truck's next trip (a new Gate In = a new trip).
6. **Delivery failed / Canceled:** a reason is required.

A ⚠️ "Please check" box explains anything unusual (wrong order, DN still open on another trip …): choose **Yes, record** or **No, go back**.
No signal: the step is kept on the phone (⏳ in the header) and sent by itself with the real time.
**Supervisor/Admin:** delete an entry on the trip screen (🗑 Delete + reason, kept in Deleted Records and Audit Log; times cannot be changed), Data quality check;
Admin sets user roles (More → Users and roles).

**Summary** shows today's trips, active trips, trucks inside the site, out on delivery, completed and canceled, and DN counts.
**Trips** has Active trips (by step) and Trip history (any dates). **DN** finds a DN by number, customer, truck, trip, SO or date and shows every trip that carried it.

## ថ្មី (12 Oct 2026) · New

**ខ្មែរ.** ៩.បានប្រគល់: ឈ្មោះអ្នកទទួល ហត្ថលេខាដោយម្រាមដៃ និងរូបថតរហូតដល់ ៥។ បន្ទាប់ពីប្រគល់: បង្ហាញ QR ឲ្យអតិថិជនវាយតម្លៃ (មិនចាំបាច់ចូលគណនី) ហើយតៃកុងវាយតម្លៃទីតាំងអតិថិជន។
សង្ខេប: ឡាននៅក្នុងរោងចក្រយូរ ប្តូរពណ៌ទឹកក្រូច ហើយក្រហម (ផ្ញើ Telegram)។ DN: 📣 កត់ត្រា និងដោះស្រាយបណ្តឹង, បញ្ជាក់ការយឺតដោយអតិថិជន។
ចូលថ្លឹងឡានជួល: ជ្រើសក្រុមហ៊ុន 3PL។ ផ្សេងៗ → Dashboard (OTIF, ពេលវេលាក្នុងរោងចក្រ, ពិន្ទុ 3PL) និង បណ្តឹងអតិថិជន។

**English.** Delivered now takes the receiver name, a finger signature and up to 5 photos. After Delivered, show the customer the rating QR
(no sign-in) and the driver rates the customer site. Summary lists trucks inside the plant: orange, then red (and a Telegram alert).
DN screen: 📣 add and resolve complaints, confirm a customer delay (left out of OTIF). Rented truck Gate In asks for the 3PL company.
More → Dashboard (OTIF, turnaround, 3PL scorecard; Supervisor, Admin, Logistics) and Customer complaints.
A delivery recorded more than 1 km from the customer site is flagged on the trip; Logistics can set the customer site from a trip.

## ថ្មី (12 Oct 2026, ទី២) · New

**ខ្មែរ.** ជំហានជាបញ្ជីទម្លាក់ចុះ មានពណ៌ និងរូប។ គ្រប់ចន្លោះត្រូវបំពេញ (លើកលែងតែកំណត់សំគាល់) ចន្លោះខ្វះប្តូរពណ៌ក្រហម។
ជ្រើសប្រភេទឡានមុន: ឡាន ISI បង្ហាញតែឡានក្នុង Driver & Truck (Fuel)។ ឡានជួល / ឡានអតិថិជន បង្ហាញតែឡានក្នុង 3PL Truck ហើយឡានថ្មី: ➕ បន្ថែមឡានថ្មី។ ផ្លាកលេខ: ស្វែងរកតាមលេខ (ឧ. 1234)។ រូបថតទាំងអស់មានលេខជើងដឹក និងថ្ងៃម៉ោង។ រូបថតឡាន ១–៥។ ការរក្សាទុកលឿន: រូបថតផ្ញើបន្ទាប់ (⏳ នៅលើក្បាល)។

**English.** The step is a coloured dropdown. Every field is required except the remark; what is missing turns red.
Choose the truck type first: ISI truck lists only the Fuel sheet's Driver & Truck plates; Rented / Customer truck lists only the 3PL Truck tab, and a new one is added with ➕ Add new truck. Plate search is by number (e.g. 1234); 1–5 truck photos. Every photo is stamped with the Trip ID and date-time.
Saving is fast: the step is saved first, its photos follow in the background (⏳ in the header until they are sent).
Timelines no longer show email addresses.

## On a computer, and steps already passed (12 Oct 2026)

**English.** On a computer the menu stands down the left side; ⇄ moves it to the right, ✏️ (or holding a button) lets you drag the buttons into your own order, and 📱 in the header shows the app as on a phone (🖥 goes back). These choices are kept on that computer.
A trip that has done a step is no longer offered for that step or an earlier one (e.g. a trip at Loading is not offered for step 4), and a DN that has arrived at the customer is not offered for Arrived again. The server refuses such entries too.

## One user list (17 Oct 2026)

**English.** Delivery now reads users from the Fuel Refill sheet's **Users** tab, like every other app. To give someone Delivery access: Active = Yes, tick ដឹកជញ្ជូន, and choose their **Delivery Role**. The Users screen under More edits that same tab.
