## English

**Trips and DNs.** Every Gate In makes a new Trip ID (e.g. 26-10-12-008); one truck can make several trips a day.
A trip can carry many DNs and one DN can be split over several trips. Each step is a new row; nothing is overwritten.

1. **Security – Gate In:** Record → ១.ចូលថ្លឹង → choose or type the plate (rented/customer trucks can be typed) → driver → photo (optional) → Record. The Trip ID is shown.
2. **Logistics – Arrival-Pickup DN:** choose the trip → add DNs from the Delivery Plan, by typing, or 📷 scan the Delivery Note barcode. A DN not in the plan needs the customer. Then Departure-Pickup.
3. **Warehouse – steps 4, 5, 6:** End Loading asks for the warehouse, weight, websling and a goods photo.
4. **Security – Gate Out.**
5. **Driver – Arrived / Delivered:** on the trip screen tap ▦ QR or Share link. The driver opens it (no sign-in), ticks the DN and records Arrived or Delivered.
   Part delivery: tick "Only part delivered" and type the kg. The rest goes on the truck's next trip (a new Gate In = a new trip).
6. **Delivery failed / Canceled:** a reason is required.

A ⚠️ "Please check" box explains anything unusual (wrong order, DN still open on another trip …): choose **Yes, record** or **No, go back**.
No signal: the step is kept on the phone (⏳ in the header) and sent by itself with the real time.
**Supervisor/Admin:** correct a time on the trip screen (✎ Correct time + reason, written to Audit Log), Data quality check;
Admin sets user roles (More → Users and roles).

**Summary** shows today's trips, active trips, trucks inside the site, out on delivery, completed and canceled, and DN counts.
**Trips** has Active trips (by step) and Trip history (any dates). **DN** finds a DN by number, customer, truck, trip, SO or date and shows every trip that carried it.

## ថ្មី (12 Oct 2026) · New

**ខ្មែរ.** ៩.បានប្រគល់: ឈ្មោះអ្នកទទួល ហត្ថលេខាដោយម្រាមដៃ និងរូបថតរហូតដល់ ៥។ បន្ទាប់ពីប្រគល់: បង្ហាញ QR ឲ្យអតិថិជនវាយតម្លៃ (មិនចាំបាច់ចូលគណនី) ហើយតៃកុងវាយតម្លៃទីតាំងអតិថិជន។
សង្ខេប: ឡាននៅក្នុងរោងចក្រយូរ ប្តូរពណ៌ទឹកក្រូច ហើយក្រហម (ផ្ញើ Telegram)។ DN: 📣 កត់ត្រា និងដោះស្រាយបណ្តឹង, បញ្ជាក់ការយឺតដោយអតិថិជន។
ចូលថ្លឹងឡានជួល: ជ្រើសក្រុមហ៊ុន 3PL។ ផ្សេងៗ → Dashboard (OTIF, ពេលវេលាក្នុងរោងចក្រ, ពិន្ទុ 3PL) និង បណ្តឹងអតិថិជន។

**English.** Delivered now takes the receiver name, a finger signature and up to 5 photos. After Delivered, show the customer the rating QR
(no sign-in) and the driver rates the customer site. Summary lists trucks inside the plant: orange, then red (and a Telegram alert).
DN screen: 📣 add and resolve complaints, confirm a customer delay (left out of OTIF). Rented truck Gate In asks for the 3PL company.
More → Dashboard (OTIF, turnaround, 3PL scorecard; Supervisor, Admin, Logistics) and Customer complaints.
A delivery recorded more than 1 km from the customer site is flagged on the trip; Logistics can set the customer site from a trip.
